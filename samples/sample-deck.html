Deckhand

A presentation you and your AI edit together

This deck is one plain HTML file. Change it by hand, leave notes for your agent, and every edit is written straight back to the file.

Try it here

Edit by hand

  • Double-click any text to rewrite it.
  • Drag anything to move it.
  • Paste or drop an image, then pull a corner to resize it.
  • Cmd or Ctrl + Z undoes. Add Shift to redo.

02

Everything else

Leave a note

Right-click anything and tell the AI what you want, in your own words. Tick ‘Apply to all similar elements’ when a change should reach every heading or every list.

  • Make this title warmer and a little bigger This heading
  • Turn these points into two columns This list
  • Set every heading in a serif All similar

03

When you're ready

Hand off

  1. One clickYour agent reads the deck and the work order, then applies every note.
  2. Watch it workThe deck is read-only while it runs, and each step shows up as it happens.
  3. Stop or RevertStop at any moment. Revert puts the deck and your notes back exactly as they were.

04

It's still just an HTML file

Open it in any browser to present. Send it to anyone. Your notes live next to it, never inside it.

Right-click anything and ask. That's the whole idea.

  • your-deck.htmlThe deck itself. Slides, styles and text.
  • your-deck.deckhand.jsonYour notes and layout tweaks.
  • your-deck.deckhand.mdThe work order your agent reads.
  • deckhand-assets/Images you paste or drop in.
